Program Details

Citation 500 Series Type Rating

Our comprehensive program prepares you to operate Citation 500 series aircraft with confidence and precision. The course combines advanced simulator training with actual flight experience.

  • FAA Part 142 Certified Training
  • 40 hours of full-motion simulator training
  • 15 hours of actual flight training
  • Ground school with systems integration
  • Single Pilot Exemption training
  • High-altitude operations certification
  • RVSM and RNP AR approved training
  • Post-graduation job placement assistance
